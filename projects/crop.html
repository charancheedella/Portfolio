Crop Vision

A concept for helping farmers monitor crop health, manage resources and improve yields.

  • No.07 of 07
  • TypeConcept
  • Built withDrones and IoT sensors · AI/ML models · Mobile and web dashboard · Firebase
  • StatusConcept

The idea

A conceptual project about agricultural productivity and sustainability. The idea is to give farmers tools and knowledge to monitor crop health, manage resources and improve yields, planned out as a prototype solution.

What it does

  • Identify problems from field data and farmer input.
  • Monitor crop health and growth in real time.
  • Combine weather predictions with smart irrigation logic.
  • Guidance on fertiliser schedules and pest control.
  • A focus on sustainable farming and community awareness.

Proposed stack

  • Drones and IoT sensors
  • AI/ML models
  • Mobile and web dashboard
  • Firebase

What I learned

  • Understanding the key problems farmers face.
  • Planning scalable, tech-driven solutions for rural problems.
  • Working with others on a proposal for real-world impact.
  • Writing a clear problem statement and a prototype plan.
