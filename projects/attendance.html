Attendance Tracker

Works out your attendance percentage and tells you whether you still meet the 75% eligibility rule.

The idea

A simple web app for students: enter your sessions and it calculates your attendance percentage, checks it against the 75% eligibility rule, and tells you how many classes you need to get back above it.

What it does

  • Enter total sessions, classes attended and absences.
  • Calculates the attendance percentage.
  • Suggests how many classes you need to reach 75%.
  • A simple, friendly interface.
  • A contact form for feedback.
